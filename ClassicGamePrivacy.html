In-App Review

After you have played several games, the App may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating dialog using the Google Play In-App Review API. The dialog and any rating or review you submit are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; the App does not receive your rating, review text, or Google account information. To avoid asking too often, the App only stores, on your device, the number of games you have completed, the date of the last request, the app version, and a request counter.

Consent for Ads

In regions where it is required by law (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads are requested or personalized. Advertising is only initialized after consent rules permit it, and until consent is given, the App does not request ads. Where applicable, you can review or change your choice at any time through the "Privacy options" button in the in-game Settings screen. Your consent choice is stored on your device by the Google UMP SDK.
