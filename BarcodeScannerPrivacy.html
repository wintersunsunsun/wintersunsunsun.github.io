Effective Date: March 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Barcode Scanner ("we," "our," or "the App") handles your information. The App is developed by Bamboo Grove Studio as an Ad-supported app. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection and Use

User Content and Local Data Processing

All user content, including scanned barcode data, camera feeds, scan history, and photos selected from your library, is processed locally using Google ML Kit and stored in the app’s local database. We do not collect, upload, store, or share your scanned content or photos on any external servers.

Third-Party Service Providers

The App utilizes third-party SDKs and services for crash reporting, advertising, consent management, and system integrations. These third-party service providers may automatically collect certain diagnostic and usage data governed by their respective Privacy Policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play In-App Review
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • AdMob
  • Meta Audience Network
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google ML Kit

2. Consent Management and Ad Personalization

In accordance with regional data protection regulations (such as GDPR in the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland) and Google policies, the App utilizes the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage user consent for ad personalization and data processing. Upon initial startup, users are presented with a consent form when required. Users may review or update their ad consent preferences at any time through the "Privacy Options" entry in the App's settings (displayed when required by regional regulations).

When a user consents to personalized ads, personalized ads are displayed. If a user rejects personalized ads, the App will still display non-personalized ads (NPA) or limited ads as determined automatically by the UMP and AdMob SDKs, provided that ad requests are permitted. Ads are only suppressed when ad requests are restricted (such as when consent is incomplete).

3. Technical and Diagnostic Log Data

Whenever you use our Service, in the event of an app error or crash, diagnostic information called Log Data is collected automatically through third-party SDKs (such as Firebase Crashlytics and AdMob). This Log Data does not contain your scanned content or personal identifiers, but may include technical statistics such as your device's Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, app configuration at the time of the event, date and time of usage, and crash stack traces to help us debug and improve app stability.

4. Cookies

Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. These are sent to your browser from the websites that you visit and are stored on your device's internal memory.

This Service does not use these “cookies” explicitly. However, the app may use third-party code and libraries that use “cookies” to collect information and improve their services.
