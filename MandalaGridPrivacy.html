Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Mandala Grid (hereinafter referred to as "this Application") collects, uses, and protects your information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy is intended to explain how we handle information collected through this Application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Personal Data

This Application will not require you to create an account, nor will it actively collect your personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, or phone number).

Local Data Storage and On-Device AI Integration (AppFunctions)

  • Local Data Storage: All content you create in the Application (such as Mandala grid titles, sub-topics, cell content, and notes) is stored in a local database on your device (using Room Persistence Library). We do not operate external servers to access, upload, or backup your content. If you delete the Application, this local data will be lost unless you have performed a system-level backup.
  • Android AppFunctions Integration (v1.3.0+): This Application supports Android AppFunctions (androidx.appfunctions). This feature allows on-device system AI assistants (such as Gemini or Google Assistant on Android 16+) to search, inspect, create, or update your Mandala grids locally upon your voice or text command. All AppFunctions processing is executed strictly on your device ("On-Device"), and your content is never transmitted to or stored on developer servers.

Third-Party Services

In order to provide advertising services, analytics, and crash reporting, this Application uses third-party services. These services may collect information used to identify your device or analyze usage.

Third-party service providers used by this Application and links to their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob
  • Meta Audience Network
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics

2. Information Handled by Third-Party SDKs

Depending on the third-party services integrated, the following types of non-personally identifiable information may be collected or processed:

  • Device or Other Identifiers: Advertising ID, device model, operating system version, and app instance IDs.
  • App Activity: Feature usage events (such as grid count, share to AI, image/PDF exports) for analytics.
  • Crash Logs and Diagnostics: Stack traces, app state, and diagnostic logs when an unexpected error occurs.
  • Coarse Location: Approximate location data derived from IP address used by ad networks (AdMob, Meta Audience Network) for ad serving.

3. Log Data

When an error occurs while using this Application, diagnostic information and crash reports may be collected automatically through third-party services (such as Firebase Crashlytics). This data helps us identify and fix bugs to improve application stability.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are files containing a small amount of data that are typically used as anonymous unique identifiers. This Application does not explicitly use traditional web Cookies. However, third-party SDKs (such as AdMob, Meta Audience Network, and Firebase) may use anonymous identifiers or internal mechanisms to collect non-personal information to optimize ads and analytics.

5. Service Providers

We may employ third-party companies and individuals for the following reasons:

  • To facilitate our service;
  • To provide the service on our behalf;
  • To perform service-related services; or
  • To assist us in analyzing how our service is used.

These third parties have access to non-personally identifiable device information solely to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

6. Security

We value your trust in providing us with information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. All data transmitted between the Application and third-party SDKs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS). However, remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

This service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Therefore, we recommend that you check this page periodically for changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us.
